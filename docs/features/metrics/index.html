Metrics

On this page
  • The charts
  • Keeping metrics up to date
  • Configuration
  • Related

Metrics turn your library into charts on the dashboard, so you can see what your collection is made of and how well tagged it is.

The charts#

ChartWhat it shows
OverviewHow many tracks, artists and albums you have.
Release yearsHow many tracks were released each year, from 1900 onwards.
GenresHow your tracks are spread across genres. Bigger tiles mean more tracks.
FormatsThe share of each audio format (MP3, FLAC, …) in your library.
Metadata completenessThe percentage of tracks that have an ISRC, BPM, year, genre and lyrics.

The metadata completeness chart is a quick way to see what needs work: a low Lyrics bar means it’s time for a lyrics analysis, and low Year or Genre bars point to tracks worth fixing in the tag editor.

Keeping metrics up to date#

Most charts are calculated once and saved, so the dashboard stays fast even with a large library. This means they don’t update by themselves as your library changes.

Click Refresh Metrics on the dashboard to recalculate them. This runs in the background as a job; the charts show the new numbers once it finishes. Refresh after a big import, a merge, or a lyrics analysis.

The metadata completeness chart is always calculated live, so it’s always current.

Configuration#

Metrics have no settings.

Related#

  • UI / Dashboard: where the charts are shown.
  • Library: the collection the metrics describe.
  • Lyrics and Metadata: improve the completeness numbers.
